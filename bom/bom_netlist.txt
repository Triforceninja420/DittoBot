Hold current after pulse = 0. Nd disc provides residual hold. Coil releases only.
Pulse bank is polypropylene film, charged to 36 V nominal (do not exceed ~42 V into the DRV8871).
Measure magnet, turns, R, peak I, ON N, OFF N, pin engagement, temperature. Paper numbers are retired.
Thermal governor in firmware must refuse sustained duty if plate temperature is high.
