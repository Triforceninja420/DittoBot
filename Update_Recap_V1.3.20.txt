DittoBot / Voxel Box Bot Update – November 5, 2026

Design owned by Craig. Started Nov/Dec 2025. Programmable matter.

Lack of a personal shop does not kill it.

Published exact coil geometry and force-fixture design so any makerspace,
university lab, or hobbyist with wire, a scale, and ten minutes can generate
the locked Phase 0 force numbers.

New files (Independent Fab Package addition, zero impact on locked criteria):
- winding/DittoBot_Phase0_Exact_Coil_Geometry.txt
  Pole pieces 2.00 × 9.00 × 11.00 mm, rods side-by-side, winding window,
  lead routing, measurement list.
- CAD/DittoBot_Phase0_Force_Fixture.scad
  Printable clamp + steel-target alignment jig.
- winding/DittoBot_Phase0_Ten_Minute_Force_Fixture.txt
  Sequence: wind, pulse, pull, photograph, publish.

Locked Phase 0 remains frozen:
  ON ≥ 3.0 N at zero gap, OFF < 0.3 N, 160–200 turns, 2.5–6 Ω,
  220 µF / 30–36 V cap-dump, IRLZ44N class, zero hold current after switch.

Once measured numbers exist the lattice leaves paper. Credit follows the data.
