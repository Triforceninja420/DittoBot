# DB-MARS-CONV-002: High-Output Converter Spec v1.1

**Executive Summary:** Vertical tower stacks at 1,100 °C instead of flat panels at 800 °C. Same 200 TW budget, but ~2,700 towers (150 m tall) instead of 4×10⁶ flat units. Projected ~112 Earth years — but honest flag: sim rerun showed ~0.38×10⁹ kg O₂/sol, giving 37,000 Earth years, not 112. The 112-year figure was an overshoot. 26-million-fold shortfall stands. Tower count or per-unit output must increase dramatically. This is the lesson that drives v3 (50×) and v4 (distributed matrix).

**Design Owner:** Craig. Paper/simulation only.

## 3. Honest Flag

Sim rerun: towers produce ~0.38×10⁹ kg O₂/sol vs 5.25×10¹⁵ kg target = 37,000-Earth-year timeline. 112-year claim retracted. Real math: 26-million-fold shortfall. Per-unit output or unit count must rise by orders of magnitude.
