# DB-MARS-CONV-004: CO2-to-O2 Converter Spec v4.0

## Overview
Solid towers don't scale to planetary O2 — 50 billion of them would eat 10% of the planet's top-meter mass. Pivot to distributed catalytic matrix.

## Architecture Change
- Thin-film perovskite on 1-mm sintered regolith sheets
- 20 kg per unit instead of 1,000 tonnes
- 50 billion units cover 500,000 km2 (~0.3% of Mars)
- 1 PW draw, inside governor cap
- Fleet mass: 1 billion tonnes (down from 1.8 million... wait, up from 1.8M tonnes to 1B tonnes but per-unit is 20kg)
- Fab energy: 40-80 TWh, swarm covers in under a day at peak

## Ugly Flag
Total CO2 in Mars atmosphere: ~25 trillion tonnes. Splitting it all yields ~13 trillion tonnes O2. As CO2 burns down, pressure collapses, greenhouse dies, Mars freezes before finishing.
