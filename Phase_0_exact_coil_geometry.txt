LOCKED PARENT NUMBERS (unchanged)
  Magnets     AlNiCo 5 Ø5.0 × 8.0 mm axial  +  NdFeB N42/N35 Ø5.0 × 8.0 mm axial
  Wire        34 AWG (0.16 mm) preferred / 32 AWG (0.20 mm) accept
  Turns       160–200 (target 180 first articles)
  Outer Ø     < 8.5 mm
  Cold R      2.5–6 Ω
  ON force    ≥ 3.0 N at zero gap on mild steel
  OFF residual < 0.3 N
  Pulse       220 µF @ 30–36 V, peak I ≥ 4 A measured (6–10 A target)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXACT GEOMETRY (first-article H-core / side-by-side rods)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All dimensions in millimeters. Tolerances are for hand or makerspace fab.

POLE PIECES (2 per EPM)
  Material          mild steel / 1018 / Q235
  Thickness         2.00 ± 0.10
  Height            9.00 ± 0.20   (matches 8.0 mm rods + 0.5 mm proud each end)
  Width             11.00 ± 0.20
  Working face      sanded flat 400–600 grit, edges lightly broken
  Back face         flat enough to clamp

ROD PLACEMENT (between the two poles)
  Rods side-by-side, long axes parallel, centers 5.5 mm apart.
  AlNiCo rod        centered in the width, axial magnetization.
  NdFeB rod         next to AlNiCo, same axial direction for first articles.
  Ends              flush with working pole face or 0.10–0.20 shy.
  Bond              thin epoxy film only. No thick fillets on the working face.
  Finished working face diameter presented to steel ≤ 5.0 mm.

WINDING WINDOW
  Primary wind around the AlNiCo rod only (first articles).
  Optional: wind around both rods together (still acceptable).
  Mandrel barrier   one layer thin Kapton or enamel tape on the AlNiCo.
  Layer height      keep total build so finished coil OD < 8.5 mm.
  Typical 34 AWG × 180 turns, 4 layers:
    Layer 1 ~45–50 turns
    Layer 2 ~45–50 turns
    Layer 3 ~45–50 turns
    Layer 4 remainder
  Lead exit         40–60 mm flying leads (28–30 AWG) strain-relieved under first 8 turns.
  Same winding sense on all four face EPMs relative to the working face.

LEAD ROUTING
  Leads leave the non-working end or the side, never across the working face.
  Optional Ø1.20 mm plate exits are already in the face-plate CAD.
