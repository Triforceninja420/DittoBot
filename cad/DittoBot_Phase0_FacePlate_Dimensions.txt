DittoBot Phase 0 Face — Dimension Table (rev 2026-11-24, 20 mm redesign)
20 mm face. All dimensions mm unless noted.
Origin: face center, Z out of docking face. Export STL from SCAD for fab.
Monte Carlo (100k trials): all-pass 98.04%, T3 99.30%, 702 T3 fails (LAT_OFFSET_SIGMA 0.55).
Full geometry follows cad/voxel_cell_phase0_20mm.scad — this table tracks the
locked spec numbers; corner-mount centers were re-derived for the 20 mm
envelope in the CAD redesign and are authoritative in the SCAD.
Cams deferred to Option A / 25 mm cell (rev 2026-11-23b); Phase 0 release
is the Option A sliding-pin architecture per docs/OPTION_A_RELEASE_SPEC.md.

Dock durability (Jaden thermal model, 8-yr dust-static tau):
  Baseline:        unlock 5.39 yrs, tear 10.78 yrs
  Wiper alone:     tear 20.99 yrs (wiper removes >10% fouling per cycle)
  Stacked:         tear 22.06 yrs, unlock 11.03 yrs
  Wiper is primary; interlock is retention insurance if magnet degrades.
